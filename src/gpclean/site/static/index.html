Keyboard shortcuts

j / k
next / previous item
space
show or hide the large preview
x
select or unselect the item (shift-click selects a range)
a
approve (To delete) or add the selection to the queue (Junk, Search)
r
reject
u
undo a decision (back to proposed, or remove your own addition)
o
open the item in Google Photos (a new tab; close it with Ctrl+W after pressing #)
d
deletion mode: mark deleted and move to the next item
1-9
Duplicates: make member N the keeper of the current group
?
show or hide this help
Esc
close preview and help

In Google Photos, press # (Shift+3) to move the open photo to the trash.
